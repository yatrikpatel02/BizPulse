================================================================================
BIZPULSE - BUSINESS HEALTH VS. ANALYTICS HEALTH INDEX
================================================================================

If a faculty member or reviewer asks: 
"What is the difference between the Dashboard's Business Health score and the 
Analytics page's Business Health Index?"

Here is the exact distinction, including the algorithms and purposes of each:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DASHBOARD PAGE - "BUSINESS HEALTH" (Descriptive / Historical / Real-Time)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* What it represents:
  A real-time diagnostic health check of the business based on historical data 
  (what has happened up to today).

* Calculation Method:
  It is a frontend-centric, rule-based heuristic that calculates a score from 
  0 to 100 based on:
    - Base Score: Starts at 70 and scales with average customer review ratings.
      Formula: Base = 70 + (Average Rating - 3) * 10
    - Growth Bonus: Adds a bonus proportional to the business's current 
      historical revenue growth.
      Formula: Bonus = min(15, Revenue Growth % * 0.5)
    - Limits: Clamped strictly between 30 and 100.

* Purpose & UX:
  Acts as a rapid monitoring KPI card. It answer the owner's immediate question: 
  "How is my business holding up today based on current customer satisfaction 
  and revenue performance?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ANALYTICS PAGE - "ACTUAL VS. PREDICTED HEALTH" (Diagnostic vs. Predictive)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the "Forecasts & AI" tab of the Analytics page, the business health score is 
split into a side-by-side comparison:

A. Actual Business Health (Left Gauge):
   - What it represents: A deeper, backend-calculated baseline score of the 
     company's actual current performance.
   - Calculation Method: Powered by the backend `BusinessHealthService`, which 
     normalizes and computes a weighted index across 5 operational dimensions:
       1. Revenue Growth (25% Weight)
       2. Inventory Health / In-Stock Ratio (20% Weight)
       3. Customer Sentiment Rating (20% Weight)
       4. Sales Quantity Trend (20% Weight)
       5. Google Trends Market Demand (15% Weight)
   - Category Range:
       - Excellent (80 - 100)
       - Good (60 - 79)
       - Average (40 - 59)
       - Poor (0 - 39)

B. Predicted Business Health (Right Gauge):
   - What it represents: A forward-looking, machine-learning-projected health 
     score for the upcoming month.
   - Calculation Method: Powered by the backend ML pipeline (Prophet and SARIMA). 
     Instead of utilizing historical metrics, it feeds forecasted sales 
     revenues, forecasted product demands, and forecasted stockout probabilities 
     for the upcoming 30 days into the `BusinessHealthService` formula.
   - Purpose & UX:
     Acts as a proactive planning tool. It allows business owners to preview 
     the future impact of their inventory burn rates or demand shifts before 
     they manifest, warning them if health will decline into a critical zone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY COMPARISON MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature           Dashboard Health           Analytics Actual Health    Analytics Predicted Health
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data Source       Historical/Current         Historical/Current         Forward-Looking Forecasts
Logic Type        Frontend Heuristic         Weighted Backend Service   ML Pipeline (Prophet/SARIMA)
Dimensions        2 (Reviews, Growth)        5 (Rev, Inv, Reviews,      5 (Forecasted Rev, Inv, 
                                             Sales, Google Trends)      Reviews, Sales, Google Trends)
Purpose           Daily Monitoring           Deep Performance Audit     Preemptive Planning & Risk
                                                                        Mitigation
